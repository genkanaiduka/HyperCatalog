User Guide & Core Workflow

1

Scan & Index Your Drives

Click the "Scan Drive" button in the top navigation bar. Select any connected external HDD, SSD, SD card, or folder. HyperCatalog rapidly indexes file structures, sizes, extensions, and timestamps in seconds.

2

Browse Disconnected Drives in Column View

Once indexed, you can safely unplug your physical drives and put them back in storage. Select any drive from the left sidebar to navigate folder hierarchies in smooth macOS Finder Column View anytime, anywhere.

3

Instant Spotlight Search Across All Disks (⌘K)

Press ⌘K to launch the global command palette. Search millions of files across dozens of offline drives simultaneously in milliseconds. Results indicate exactly which physical drive and shelf box stores the file.

4

Save & Share Portable .hcat Catalog Files

Click "Save" (⌘S) to save all registered disks into a lightweight, standalone .hcat file. Send it via AirDrop or email to colleagues so they can search your archive without needing the physical hard drives.

Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

Q: What drive formats and file systems are supported?

HyperCatalog supports all file systems readable by macOS, including APFS, Mac OS Extended (HFS+), ExFAT, FAT32, and read-accessible NTFS drives.

Q: Can I import existing catalog files from other software?

Yes! Click the "Import CSV" button in the left sidebar to import catalog export files from Virtual Volumes View, DiskCatalogMaker, or custom CSV spreadsheets. HyperCatalog automatically detects character encodings (UTF-8, Shift-JIS, etc.).

Q: Is any personal data, file content, or telemetry sent to the cloud?

No, absolutely not. HyperCatalog is designed with a strict 100% on-device, offline-first philosophy. Your filenames, directory trees, and metadata remain strictly on your Mac. No analytics, tracking, or cloud uploads take place.

Q: Why do I get a permission error when trying to scan an external drive?

On macOS, make sure HyperCatalog has access permissions: open System Settings > Privacy & Security > Files and Folders, and ensure Removable Volumes is toggled ON for HyperCatalog.
