Privacy Policy

Effective Date: October 16, 2026 • Version 1.0

1. Overview & Commitment to Privacy

HyperCatalog ("the Application") is developed and maintained by GEN KANAIZUKA as a standalone macOS utility for indexing and browsing offline external drives and storage volumes.

Your privacy is our highest priority. HyperCatalog is architected with a strict privacy-first principle: we collect zero personal data, track zero user behavior, and transmit no file records or disk contents to any external server or third party.

2. Data Collection: None

When you use HyperCatalog, we do not collect:

  • Personally identifiable information (such as your name, email, IP address, or Apple ID).
  • File contents, filenames, folder hierarchies, or storage metadata.
  • Usage statistics, session duration, device identifiers, or telemetry.
  • Crash logs or diagnostics unless you explicitly choose to attach them in an email to our support desk.

3. On-Device Storage & Local Sandboxing

All disk indexing operations take place entirely on your Mac. Metadata such as folder names, file sizes, modification timestamps, and file extension categories are stored exclusively within:

  • The application's isolated macOS App Sandbox container.
  • Local portable catalog files (.hcat) or spreadsheets (.csv) explicitly created or exported by you.

No catalog information is ever uploaded to the cloud, synchronized with third-party servers, or shared with external entities.

4. Network Activity & Local IPC Loopback

HyperCatalog operates completely offline. The application uses a local loopback network interface (127.0.0.1) solely for internal Inter-Process Communication (IPC) between the native user interface and the local index search engine running on your Mac.

No packets or requests leave your computer. The application functions flawlessly with Wi-Fi, Ethernet, and all network connections completely disabled.

5. Third-Party Services, SDKs & Advertising

HyperCatalog contains:

  • No advertising networks or SDKs.
  • No third-party analytics (e.g., Google Analytics, Mixpanel, Firebase).
  • No user tracking cookies or behavioral fingerprinting.

6. File System Permissions & Disk Access

In accordance with macOS security requirements, HyperCatalog will prompt you for permission before indexing a connected volume or removable disk. HyperCatalog only reads file metadata (names, sizes, directory paths, and dates) necessary to construct the catalog. It never alters, overwrites, or deletes your physical files.

7. Children's Privacy

Because HyperCatalog collects no data whatsoever, it complies fully with the Children's Online Privacy Protection Act (COPPA), GDPR, and applicable privacy regulations worldwide.

8. Policy Updates

If this Privacy Policy is updated, changes will be posted to this page with an updated effective date. Given our strict offline design, our commitment to zero data collection remains permanent.
